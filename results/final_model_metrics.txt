Threshold Selection Reason
---------------------------
A threshold of 0.15 was selected because it achieved the
highest F1-score among the evaluated thresholds while
substantially improving recall for the minority default class.

Business Interpretation
-----------------------
The model prioritizes identifying potential loan defaults
rather than maximizing overall accuracy. This is important
in credit-risk assessment because missing a potential
defaulter can be more costly than incorrectly flagging
a low-risk customer.
